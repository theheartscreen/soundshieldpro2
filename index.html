What "untested, sold as-is" actually means

Unused, not certified

This is unsold distributor inventory that has sat in storage. Boxes do not prove an item works, and we do not claim they do. Nothing here is described as new, tested, refurbished or complete.

What is included

Each listing states exactly what is in the box and, just as importantly, what is not — headset only, no base, no controller, cords in packs of 25, cushions in packs of 50.

Battery and pairing risk

Wireless systems and cordless handsets may hold aged batteries and may need re-pairing to a base. Assume battery replacement for anything with a cell in it.

Returns

Sold as-is with no implied warranty. If an item is materially different from its listing, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we will make it right.
